Tracea Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 13, 2026

Overview

Tracea helps users inspect and remove common metadata before sharing photos, PDFs, and videos. The app is designed for local processing.

Data Collection

Tracea does not collect personal data, does not track users, and does not require an account.

File Processing

  • Selected files are processed on device.
  • Tracea does not upload, host, sell, or share user files.
  • Original files are not modified. Tracea creates clean copies only when requested by the user.

Photos and Files Access

Tracea requests access only so users can select files to scan, clean, save, or share. Access is used for user-requested actions.

In-App Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Tracea does not receive payment card details.
